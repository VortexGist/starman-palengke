About Starman Palengke:
Starman Palengke. The palengke originated as a small local market for just everyday neccessities. As time passed, the locals who were seeking to earn some cash decided also to sell daily necessities. That us how the market came to be as it is now. 
The local market is located at Quezon City where everyday locals always go to get necessities. The local citizens who sells in the market sells items/foods that are general or necessary for everyday use — generally to any other local markets.
People go here everyday to shop mostly for the fresh foods, the cheap prices per kilo, and of course for the daily necessity. The palengke consists of what you generally see at most; Vegetables, Fruits, Fishes, Poultry, Eggs, Grains and Rice, etc...
The things sold at the palengke are sold at fair prices. Everything sold at Starman Palengke is made sure that everything is fresh.
